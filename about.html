Our Story.

Good pizza starts with good ingredients, but great pizza starts with people who care.

Why We Started

Pizza with personality.

Charred Apizza was created to bring something different to Springfield: Detroit-style pizza with a focus on crispy edges, soft dough, and big flavor.

Co-owners Dave Lucht and Terry McKay brought their vision to downtown Springfield and opened the brick-and-mortar restaurant in 2026.

The goal is simple: make food people love and create a place where people want to spend time.
